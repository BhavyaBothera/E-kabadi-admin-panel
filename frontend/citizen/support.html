Every collector partner is equipped with an E-Kabaadi verified, Bluetooth-synced digital platform scale. When weight is measured, the exact grams are transmitted live to your app screen before payment confirmation. Analog spring scales are strictly prohibited on the platform.

No. E-Kabaadi maintains a zero-penalty cancellation policy. You can cancel or reschedule freely anytime before the collector arrives at your gate.
